Our Story

Our Story

Founded in 2025 by refugees and individuals from refugee and immigrant backgrounds, the Refugee & Immigrant-Led Organizations Coalition (RILOC) is a nonprofit coalition first established in North Carolina, United States, with a vision to expand its impact nationwide. RILOC is built on the belief that people with lived refugee and immigrant experiences are best positioned to lead meaningful and sustainable change in their communities.

Through leadership development, capacity building, advocacy, economic empowerment, and strategic partnerships, RILOC strengthens refugee- and immigrant-led organizations while advancing equity, resilience, and community integration. By empowering community-led solutions and fostering collaboration, RILOC creates lasting opportunities for refugees, immigrants, and other underserved populations, helping build inclusive communities where everyone has the opportunity to thrive..

Our Mission

To empower refugee and immigrant-led organizations and the communities they serve through capacity building, leadership development, economic empowerment, advocacy, and strategic partnerships.

Our Vision

To create inclusive, resilient, and empowered communities where refugee and immigrant-led organizations have the capacity, resources, and leadership necessary to shape their own futures and contribute meaningfully to society.

Our Approach

Listen → Connect → Strengthen → Collaborate → Amplify

RILOC uses a community-led and coalition-based approach. Rather than imposing solutions from outside communities, we seek to work alongside refugee and immigrant-led organizations to identify needs, develop organizational capacity, share resources, build partnerships, and create sustainable change.

  • Listen to communities and understand their unique challenges and aspirations.
  • Connect organizations with resources and one another.
  • Strengthen organizational capacity through training and support.
  • Collaborate across sectors to build collective power.
  • Amplify community-led solutions and voices.
Strategic Priorities

Seven Pillars of Our Work

1

Organizational Capacity Building — Strengthening internal systems, governance, administration, financial management, strategic planning, program development, fundraising, communications, and evaluation.

2

Leadership Development — Supporting emerging and established leaders through training, mentorship, networking, and collaborative leadership.

3

Economic Empowerment & Entrepreneurship — Promoting economic opportunity through entrepreneurship initiatives, workforce development, financial literacy, and business education.

4

Youth Education & Mentorship — Equipping young people with leadership skills, educational opportunities, mentorship, and pathways toward future success.

5

Community Integration & Social Cohesion — Promoting meaningful relationships among refugee, immigrant, and U.S.-born communities through cultural exchange and inclusion.

6

Civic Engagement & Legal Awareness — Promoting civic participation and helping communities understand resources, rights, and responsibilities.

7

Advocacy & Strategic Partnerships — Elevating community voices and connecting organizations with nonprofit, governmental, philanthropic, academic, corporate, and community partners.

Our Journey

Milestones Along the Way

A coalition built step by step, alongside the communities we serve.

2025 — Coalition Founded

Immigrants, refugees, and U.S. citizens in North Carolina come together to establish RILOC as a consortium focused on strengthening the organizations already rooted within refugee and immigrant communities.

Consortium Perspective Established

RILOC adopts its core operating model: connect, strengthen, empower, and sustain — helping organizations bridge gaps in capacity, funding, and leadership.

Strategic Priorities Defined

Seven pillars of work are established: capacity building, leadership development, economic empowerment, youth education, community integration, civic engagement, and advocacy.

Coalition Network Grows

Partnerships expand with foundations, government agencies, universities, nonprofits, and faith-based organizations across North Carolina.

Today & Beyond

RILOC continues to build a collaborative network capable of sharing knowledge, resources, and opportunities across communities — with a vision to expand across the United States.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Contact our team.

What is RILOC's consortium model?

RILOC functions as a network and coalition, creating opportunities for organizations to learn from one another rather than working in isolation. Members benefit from shared knowledge, capacity-building, collaborative fundraising, resource sharing, peer learning, joint initiatives, and collective advocacy.

Who does RILOC serve?

Our primary beneficiaries include refugee-led organizations, immigrant-led organizations, community-based organizations, emerging grassroots organizations, refugee and immigrant leaders, youth and young adults, underserved communities, and families experiencing barriers to opportunity.

How is RILOC funded?

RILOC seeks a diversified funding model including foundation grants, government grants and contracts, corporate partnerships, individual philanthropy, membership contributions, program-related funding, strategic partnerships, community fundraising, and collaborative grant opportunities.

Can my organization become a coalition partner?

Yes. Visit our Partners page to learn about partnership opportunities with foundations, government agencies, universities, nonprofits, faith-based organizations, corporations, and community foundations.

What challenges does RILOC address?

Refugee and immigrant-led organizations often possess deep community knowledge yet face barriers to organizational capacity, sustainable funding, leadership development, networking, program evaluation, institutional visibility, and economic opportunity. RILOC exists to strengthen these organizations.
